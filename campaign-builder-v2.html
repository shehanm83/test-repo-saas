Campaign Builder v2

Quick Create makes a post. Campaign Builder should make a month — a dated, multi-phase plan with every asset produced on brand, in every format, stills and video, plus the words to post them with.

Status Design for review Date 22 Aug 2026 Branch redesign-2026 Supersedes slice 55, slice 57
00

The problem, and the two ideas

Campaign Builder today is a wizard-shaped skin over Quick Create. Eight steps collect more fields and then emit the byte-identical single-generation payload. Everything that reads as “campaign” in the UI is inert.

This document removes that mock and designs the real thing. It rests on one product idea and one architectural idea.

The product idea

A campaign is not a bigger generation — it is a plan. A small business does not want twelve images. It wants to know what to post, on which day, on which platform, with which words, for the next three weeks, and to have all of it made in one sitting. Every competitor either generates assets without a plan, or plans without producing brand-exact assets. Owning both halves is the wedge.

The architectural idea

Campaign consistency comes from a style contract anchored to one approved image, not from templates. The user approves one hero still; every other asset — every format, every phase, every video — is generated with that still as an image-to-image reference under a frozen contract. packages/gateway/src/routing.ts already promotes to an i2i-capable model when a reference is present. Template-based competitors structurally cannot copy this, and the 2026 video research says it is also the only reliable way to stop a product drifting across shots.

Video is not a new pipeline. It is the still we already got right, put into motion.

Decisions taken

End of flow

Calendar + export

No OAuth publishing in v1. Direct publishing stays a clean Phase 5 seam the data model already supports.

Video depth

Both, one data model

Single-clip motion and multi-scene storyboards designed together; clip-level ships first.

Copy

In scope

Captions, hooks, hashtags and CTAs are part of the deliverable. Reuses the existing caption pipeline.

One generation is one image size

Existing defect

PLATFORM_FORMATS already advertises instagram/reel, instagram/feed_video_* and tiktok/video. Selecting any of them today returns a still image at video dimensions. That is a correctness bug shipping right now, not just a missing feature — and slice 64 repairs it.

What is good and must survive

  • Brand fidelity by construction. The model generates a background; the renderer composites the real logo, real brand fonts, real palette. The model never interprets the logo. This is the moat, and the campaign design inherits it wholesale.
  • The prompt-template system. Versioned YAML, required-variable validation, safety rules, and an overlay contract splitting renderer-owned from model-owned elements. It needs campaign templates, not a rewrite.
  • i2i promotion in routing.ts. Already substitutes an i2i-capable model when a reference is present — the style-anchor mechanism is built directly on it.
  • The overlay slot contract. Headline, price, discount, badge, CTA, legal, QR — already the right vocabulary for offer-driven campaigns.
  • captionJobs + the Anthropic text provider. The copy engine is largely wiring what exists to a campaign-shaped prompt.
02

Research

What an SMB campaign actually is

The unit of work an SMB thinks in is not “an image”. It is “the thing we are doing for the next few weeks”. Three findings shape the design.

Campaigns have phases, and the phases have different jobs. The launch literature converges on one shape: pre-launch builds context and collects signal; launch week makes the offer obvious and removes friction; post-launch answers objections with testimonials, demos and usage proof to hold momentum after the announcement spike decays. The recurring asset list is stable — teaser clips, walkthroughs, founder notes, customer quotes, comparison images, short demos, FAQ cards, proof posts. That list is generatable, and encoding it is the single highest-leverage thing we can do.

Always-on content has its own stable shape. Hero–Hub–Hygiene sets the cadence: hero moments 2–4× a year around launches and seasonal events, hub content 1–4× a month, hygiene content weekly or biweekly answering known questions. A business that isn’t launching anything still needs a campaign object — just a different recipe.

Consistency is the stated top pain, and it is an execution problem. 67% of SMBs describe their social effort as “posting into the void”; over 91% of social professionals name content creation and strategy as a negative part of the role; over 75% report being expected to do too many unrelated things at once. Inconsistent posting carries a reported 61% engagement penalty against a regular schedule.

The read

The bottleneck is not idea generation — it is production volume at acceptable quality. A tool that produces three weeks of finished, dated, on-brand posts in one sitting attacks the actual constraint. A tool that produces four beautiful images does not.

The planners don’t produce. The producers don’t plan. The platform tools only optimise inside their own walls.

Nobody owns brief → dated multi-phase plan → brand-exact assets in every format, image and video → export. We already enforce brand by construction. Layer the plan on top and the combination isn’t reachable by a template-based competitor without rebuilding their rendering stack.

Video — state of the art

The market consolidated in 2026: after Sora’s closure in March, the field is effectively Veo 3.1, Kling 3.0, Seedance 2.x and Runway Gen-4.5.

The critical finding

2026 practitioner consensus is unambiguous: storyboard every hero shot as a still first, lock the product’s shape and detail there, then feed those frames into image-to-video — never ask the model to invent the product on the fly. A reference image locks identity, style and framing from frame one. Seedance and Digen productised this as “Identity Lock” and “Style Lock” precisely because drift is the failure mode everyone hits.

We already produce a locked, brand-exact still. Our video story is not “add a video model” — it is animate the thing we already got right.

What wins in-feed

  • Short-form vertical dominates — ~78% of top-performing campaigns across Meta, TikTok and Google; 60% of marketers make it their primary format.
  • 7–15 seconds for cold traffic, hook inside the first 3 seconds. A 6-second cut delivers ~60% of a 30-second ad’s impact. Warm audiences tolerate 30–60s for objection handling and proof.
  • 85% of Meta video views happen with sound off. Assets without captions lose most of their audience by the 2-second mark. Burned-in captions are the format, not a nicety — and our renderer already owns text.
  • The reliable faceless structure is four beats: negative hook → product reveal → value → CTA, cut fast to a music bed.
  • Founder-led content reports ~4.6× ROAS against ~3.1× for product-only; UGC-style outperforms polished by ~43% on TikTok/Reels. Noted for a later avatar phase — explicitly out of scope for Phases 1–4.
03

Product design

Quick Create makes a post. Campaign Builder makes a month.

The core object

A Campaign is a first-class, persistent, resumable workspace entity — not a form submission. 

Campaign
├─ brief, goal, audience, duration, platforms
├─ brand + products (roles)
├─ StyleContract        ← frozen after hero approval; the consistency mechanism
├─ Phase[]              ← tease / launch / proof / offer / last-call
│   └─ Slot[]           ← one planned asset
│        ├─ kind        image | video
│        ├─ format      platform + format → exact px
│        ├─ angle       the job this asset does
│        ├─ Copy        caption, hook, hashtags, CTA
│        ├─ scheduledAt
│        └─ Generation? ← attached when produced
└─ exports

The Slot is the pivot. A slot exists as a plan before it exists as an asset: the user sees the campaign laid out, priced and editable before a single credit is spent. See the whole plan, then commit — that property is what separates a professional tool from a slot machine, and it is why a campaign cannot be a form submission.

The five stages

Not a wizard. Five independently revisitable stages, with the campaign persisted continuously.

  1. 1

    Brief

    One screen. Goal, products, brand, duration, platforms, and 1–3 sentences of intent. Optional offer terms and deadline. Nothing else — everything else is inferred, then shown for editing.

  2. 2

    Plan

    The AI proposes the campaign: phases with dates, and inside each phase, slots with an angle, a format, a kind and draft copy. Rendered as a calendar and a list, fully editable. No generation has happened yet; total credits are shown. This screen is the product — it is what nothing else in the category does.

  3. 3

    Look

    The visual direction, decided once. The system generates 3–4 hero candidates for the campaign’s most important slot; the user picks one. That approval freezes the StyleContract and stores the chosen image as the style anchor. Cost of this stage is one small generation, not the campaign.

  4. 4

    Board

    Production. Every slot is a card in a phase-grouped board. Generate a slot, a phase, or everything. Each result is anchored to the style anchor, so the board fills in visibly as one campaign. Per card: regenerate, alternates, edit copy, swap format, promote to video, approve.

  5. 5

    Deliver

    A dated calendar (CSV/ICS), a per-post copy sheet, and every approved asset named by platform, format and date, as a ZIP. Direct publishing is the Phase 5 seam.

Campaign recipes

Recipes replace the inert creationType enum with something that actually drives phase structure, slot mix, prompt route and copy tone.

RecipePhasesCharacteristic slot mix
Product launchtease → launch → proof → last-callteaser clip, hero reveal, feature cards, demo video, testimonial card, FAQ card, urgency post
Offer / saleannounce → reminder → last-calloffer hero with price overlay, countdown story, bundle card, expiry post
Seasonalbuild-up → peak → wind-downseasonal hero, gift-guide carousel, atmosphere clip, closing-hours card

The last earns its place on the AdCreative research: a structured angle × format matrix with exactly one variable changing per row is what performance marketers actually need, and it is trivially expressible in the slot model.

The Plan engine

Input: recipe, brief, product snapshots, brand voice notes, duration, platforms, offer terms. Output: validated JSON — phases with date ranges, slots with {angle, kind, format, durationSec?, copy, scheduledAt, rationale}.

Implemented as a YAML template (category: plan_generation) executed through the Anthropic text provider already wired in the gateway, with a Zod schema on the output. The template encodes the research directly — phase archetypes, Hero–Hub–Hygiene cadence, per-platform duration rules, captions-by-default on every video slot, and the four-beat storyboard structure. Storyboards are generated at plan time, so the Plan screen shows what the video will do before anything is rendered.

The consistency system

StyleContract is frozen at hero approval and immutable for the campaign’s life. Changing it is an explicit, warned action that invalidates the anchor.

StyleContract {
  styleAnchorGenerationVariantId   // the approved hero still
  styleAnchorS3Key
  moodId, templateFamily
  composition { background, realism, shadow, brandBlend, ... }
  palette[]                        // resolved — the actual pixels used
  seed
  promptStyleClause                // distilled natural-language style statement
  negativeClause
}

Every subsequent slot generation carries the anchor as an i2i reference, the frozen composition and mood, the distilled style and negative clauses via a new modifier.campaign_consistency template, and the same seed where the provider honours it.

LevelBehaviour
looseShared mood and palette only; no anchor reference
matched defaultAnchor as reference + frozen composition
lockedmatched + seed pinning + strict negative clause + a post-generation palette-distance check that flags drift on the card

Unlike today’s dead enum, each level maps to real behaviour.

The copy engine

Every slot gets a hook, a platform-length-aware caption, a hashtag set, a CTA, and for video the burned-in caption track. Generated with the plan — so the Plan screen is never empty — then regenerable per slot. Brand voice comes from brands.voiceNotes, already in the schema. This reuses the captionJobs pipeline and its credit accounting; the change is a campaign-shaped prompt and a slot-level attachment point.

generations gains nullable campaign_id and campaign_slot_id, so a campaign generation is still an ordinary generation — history, billing, moderation and the results view all keep working unchanged.

Slice 55 is subsumed

generation_packages as specified in slice 55 is exactly campaign_slots grouped by campaign, with style_seed/style_brief generalised into style_contract. Slice 55 should be closed as superseded rather than built.

Multi-format fan-out, done properly

Today formats[] is a lie. The honest implementation: one slot = one format = one output target, and “post this everywhere” expands into sibling slots, one per format, sharing the style anchor and the copy. Consequences:

  • Pricing is honest and per-asset, because each slot is a real generation.
  • Each format gets a background composed for its own aspect ratio instead of a crop — the whole point of the no-crop framing rule already enforced in the router.
  • The overlay is re-rendered at native dimensions, so text is never scaled or resampled.

This is more generations than a naive “resize” approach, and that is correct — the alternative is crops that violate our own framing contract, or upscaled overlay text.

Generation flow

POST /api/campaigns                  → create from brief
POST /api/campaigns/:id/plan         → run plan engine, upsert phases + slots
POST /api/campaigns/:id/hero         → generate 3–4 hero candidates
POST /api/campaigns/:id/style        → approve candidate, freeze StyleContract
POST /api/campaigns/:id/generate     → { scope: all | phase | slot[] } → enqueue
GET  /api/campaigns/:id              → campaign + phases + slots + generations
POST /api/campaigns/:id/export       → build ZIP, return signed URL

Slot generation reuses GenerationApi.create with the campaign IDs set and the style anchor injected as an inspiration reference. No fork of the generation pipeline — preflight, pricebook, ledger reserve/commit/release, moderation and fan-in all apply unchanged.

Video pipeline

A new VideoProvider interface mirrors ImageProvider, with chooseVideoProvider routing on duration, native-audio need and tier — the multi-model routing the research says is standard practice. Initial registry: Kling/Seedance as the cost tier, Veo Fast where native audio matters, Runway where camera control matters. Mock provider first, so AI_MODE=mock development keeps working.

The renderer gets one focused change: RenderInput.background becomes optional, so it can emit a transparent-alpha overlay PNG. Text, logo, price badges and CTA are then composited over video with ffmpeg — identical typography, identical brand fidelity, no second text engine.

Phase A — single clip (ships first)

Frame 0

Approved still

Brand-exact, already generated and approved on the board

Motion

i2v provider

3–8s clip; the still is literally frame zero, so identity cannot drift

Composite

ffmpeg + alpha overlay

Renderer’s transparent PNG over the clip; captions burned in

Encode

Platform MP4

H.264/AAC, faststart, per-platform bitrate

Cheapest, safest, highest-confidence path — and it immediately repairs the defect where reel and TikTok formats silently return stills.

Phase B — multi-scene storyboard (same data model)

Plan

beats[]

Hook → reveal → value → CTA, written at plan time

Stills

One per beat

All anchored to the same style anchor

Motion

One clip per beat

Each beat’s still is its own frame zero

Assemble

Concat + audio

Cuts, captions, music bed, optional voiceover → 15–30s ad

storyboard jsonb is designed now and populated by the plan engine from the outset, so Phase B is purely additive: no schema change, no API change, no re-plan.

Credits

price_book_entries gains media_kind (image | video, defaulting to image so every existing row is untouched) and duration_bucket (null | short ≤8s | standard ≤15s | long ≤30s).

Campaign-level estimation aggregates slot estimates and is shown on the Plan screen before commitment, with a per-phase breakdown and partial commit. Given a 5s clip costs $0.45–0.75 at provider prices, video multipliers must be set deliberately — a video slot is meaningfully more expensive, and the UI must never let that be a surprise.

07

Risks and open questions

RiskMitigation
Style drift under i2iProviders honour reference images unevenly. The locked level pins seed and adds a post-generation palette-distance check that surfaces drift on the card rather than shipping it silently. Needs per-model calibration before 61 ships.
Campaign cost shockA 20-slot campaign with video is a large credit event. Full estimate on the Plan screen before commitment, per-phase partial commit, hard confirmation above a threshold.
Plan quality is the whole productIf the plan is generic, the differentiator collapses. The template encodes the researched frameworks explicitly rather than asking the model to improvise a strategy. Treat it as a versioned, reviewed asset.
ffmpeg in the workerContainer weight, CPU cost, new failure surface. Isolate in renderer/src/video.ts behind the same sandbox discipline as the Puppeteer fallback; encode as a distinct job step with its own retry.
Video provider volatilityThe field consolidated once already in 2026. VideoProvider mirrors ImageProvider exactly, so a provider swap is a registry change.
Longer time-to-first-pixelCampaign Builder is slower than Quick Create by design — but the Plan screen must feel like progress, so plan generation streams and the Board fills in progressively.

Open questions

  • Do campaigns need multi-user review/approval states for the “compliance matters” personas, or is single-owner approval enough for v1?
  • Should always-on campaigns auto-roll month to month, or be re-planned each period?
  • Should the ad-test-pack recipe carry a scoring/prediction signal — the AdCreative wedge — and is that v1 or a later analytics slice?
  • Does music licensing need a cleared library, or do we ship silent-with-captions first? Given 85% sound-off, silent-with-captions is defensible for Phase A.
  • Is a campaign_slots.locale dimension worth designing now, given the platform tools are shipping one-click dubbing into 50+ languages?
